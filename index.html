The short version

I served, came home, and eventually ended up installing outdoor lighting in Central Kentucky. Three years in the trade. Evening Glow LLC — residential and commercial installs across Nicholasville, Lexington, and surrounding areas.

Along the way I got tired of how long proposals took. Three-plus hours per job, every time, writing everything from scratch. So I built a tool to fix it. That became LightDeck — an AI-powered estimating tool that gets you from job description to finished proposal in about 8 minutes.

I've got a 2-year-old at home. My work window is 10:30 AM to 1 PM most days. Everything here was built in that window — the business, the product, the systems. That constraint turned out to be a feature.

Now I'm sharing what I've learned along the way — through a free community, YouTube, and some tools I've put together that I think other contractors would find useful.

I'm not building a startup. I'm building an empire — bootstrapped, permanent, cash-flowing. The lighting business is the proof of concept. The systems business is the wealth vehicle.
