01 / What I’m building

LightDeck

Turn the details from a property walkthrough into a proposal a contractor can review.

My roleFounder & developer
Core stackJavaScript
Supabase / Postgres
Vercel

A job starts with photos, fixture locations, site notes, and pricing decisions. I’m building a workspace that keeps those details connected through planning and proposal preparation.

I work across the application, database design, automated testing, and release verification. My focus is giving AI useful context while keeping structured job data and human review in control.
